Decohere

The philosophy of identifying moments of clarity through introspection and common understandings in order to promote flourishing. Only this core statement shall remain unchanged.

Goals

Why "Decohere"? This foundation's goal is to promote human flourishing by building a philosophy of purposeful, guided, and beneficial decoherence of the shadow.

Guiding Principles

The principles laid out here form the loosely held tenets which we believe could lead to human flourishing.

Introspection

Humility

Authenticity

Accountability

Self Governance / Individuation

Collective Conscience

Not to be confused with collective consciousness or the collective unconscious, this principle lays out the idea that we are all here together, in different ways. Some of the effects of our actions may have varying impacts on individuals, groups, cultures, societies.

Alignment

Probably one of the most distinct ideas of Decohere is the idea that humanity can align. "Come together" but with an explicit, study-able, recorded meaning. This alignment doesn't need to be one. It can be many under the umbrella of Decohere.

Portability

Because the core tenets through their alignment allow it, each of them may be "picked" for use in life as we need.

Judgement

Through convention we must draw some lines in the sand, though some may be fuzzy, moving and evolving.

The Lotus of the Logos

We accept the observational findings of the occultic and hidden knowledge traditions but reject the integration of the shadow as the final, or even as a necessary step in enlightenment. Definitions

Although this philosophy has no intent to cleverly redefine any terms as a form of cultural spell-casting, it is nonetheless ineherent in building a system of thought that key terms should be described with as specific, literal and semantic as possible. Although the common/plain American English definition du jour should be assumed if there's no definition laid out otherwise. If there is a definition, note that

The core work of the initial conventions will be to mold these definitions so that by introspecting upon them and our lived experiences we may contemplate our suffering and joy to contribute to the potential flourishing. If a definition is found to have too heavy a bias against this, or a flaw in its semantics, or a construction that breaks the core tenets, it will be revised for better contextualization or excised altogether if found to be untenable. Some words are better at being self-evident, that's just how it is.
